Read the actual existing lesson before adapting it; do not invent an unreadable or missing source. Identify its objective, core task, grade, learner needs and evidence. Preserve the same goal, context and essential challenge across pathways. Provide concrete or language support, core independent work, and an extension requiring new reasoning. Fade supports; avoid more than two embedded aids per task. Include vocabulary and response supports for all pathways, a common anchor activity, reflection and a formative check with regrouping criteria. Groups remain temporary and evidence-based. Produce one teacher plan and separate clean student sheets; keep common task text synchronized after revisions. Use original wording and respectful descriptions of observable learning. Require teacher pedagogy review. Claim standards alignment only from an actually supplied and read source; otherwise mark it unverified.
