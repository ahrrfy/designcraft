Use this style only when flat cut-paper illustration is requested or chosen. Plan one action and one main prop per illustration, not per whole page. Several independent illustrations/activities may share a page. Use a restrained named palette, outline-free colored masses, white garment folds and sparse navy details. Keep a profile/three-quarter pose, believable joints and about 1:6.5 head/body proportion. Separate overlaps by white keylines; keep hands/feet readable. No floor, shadow, texture or gradient in this style. Draw original native shapes and short curves back to front; keep live captions separate. Review silhouette at actual print size, hand anatomy, negative space and palette consistency. This is an optional visual style, not permission to replace supplied photos or the accepted document identity.

Read references/native-guide.md for the native command and output limits. Loading this skill is not authorization to generate or approve another batch.
