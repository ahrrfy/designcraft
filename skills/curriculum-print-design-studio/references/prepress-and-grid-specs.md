# Exact native layout and print contract

A4 trim is 210 × 297 mm, aspect ratio 210/297 (not 3:4). Native coordinates use
points: mm × 72/25.4. School default: 15 mm safe margins, zero bleed, actual-size
100% proof. Respect the existing document and task settings. A commercial job
may request 3 mm bleed (216 × 303 mm full extent) and an 18 mm binding margin;
never silently change the accepted school profile or crop artwork to imitate it.

Set reading/binding direction explicitly. Separate Arabic RTL and English LTR
frames. Verify installed/licensed font family and style, actual glyphs, shaping,
line spacing, contrast and native overset. Keep headings, numbered actions,
caption bands, handwriting guides, page numbers and learner areas editable.
Use a consistent grid and baseline rhythm while changing body arrangements.

Use independent coloured illustrations at useful effective PPI (target 300 for
press work) without enlarging low-detail art and claiming restored resolution.
Use native fitting/crops, preserve object proportions and frame clearances.

Paper stocks, opacity, lamination and spot UV are vendor specifications, not
properties created by this skill. Do not guarantee marker bleed-through, saddle
stitch capacity or a universal ICC profile. Request printer-approved stock,
binding, CMYK/output intent and ink limits when commercial delivery requires it.
Only claim PDF/X compliance, embedded fonts, output intent, actual PPI and correct
page boxes when the exporter and final-file inspection prove them. Colour values
alone do not establish an ICC profile. Export IDML for editable interchange;
native INDD conversion remains limited by the host's installed-InDesign path.
