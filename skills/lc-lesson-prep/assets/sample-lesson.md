# Original scratch sample lesson

Grade 5 mathematics. Objective: distinguish area from perimeter.
Warm-up (5 minutes): count rows and columns in a unit grid.
Explore (12 minutes): compare a 3 cm by 8 cm rectangle and a 4 cm by 6 cm rectangle. Find each area and perimeter. Does equal area imply equal perimeter? Explain.
Discuss (8 minutes): compare counting square units with tracing the boundary.
Exit (5 minutes): draw another rectangle of area 24 square units and find its perimeter.
