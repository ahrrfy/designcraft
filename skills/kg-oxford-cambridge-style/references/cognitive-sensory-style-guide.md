- **10 Executable Page Families**: `international-curriculum-cover`, `international-scope-sequence`, `garnet-computing-systems-lab`, `oxford-vocab-story`, `oxford-phonics-maze`, `cambridge-cpa-grammar`, `oxford-song-colours`, `oxford-workbook-print`, `oxford-skills-time`, `oxford-fluency-project`.
- **100% Dynamic Text Slots**: Every single linguistic text frame on all 10 templates is bound to a `DesignTextSlot` so 100% of headings, badges, cards, speech bubbles, diagrams, and tasks adapt to the requested subject (e.g. Computer Science / الحاسوب) and language (Arabic or English).
- **Decomposable Vector Groups**: Every vector component is grouped with `object.group` (`3..=10` `part.*` atomic parts) and can be ungrouped (`object.ungroup`) for part-level editing.
